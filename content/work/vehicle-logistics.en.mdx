## The problem

Dispatchers assigned routes by phone and drivers logged deliveries on paper at the end of the day. There was no way to see where a vehicle was mid-route, and rerouting around a delay meant calling every driver individually.

## What we built

A dispatcher console paired with a driver mobile app: live GPS tracking, automatic route optimization, and push-based reassignment when a delivery window slips. Drivers confirm each stop from the app, so proof of delivery is timestamped and searchable instead of sitting in a binder.

## The result

The fleet now reroutes around delays in minutes instead of hours, and on-time delivery rate rose from 81% to 96% in the first full quarter after launch. Dispatchers manage the same fleet size with two fewer people on shift.
