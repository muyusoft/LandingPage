Concept demonstration with fictional data. It does not represent a commissioned project, a client, or measured results.

## The scenario

This example explores coordination between a dispatch console and a mobile driver interface.

## What it shows

The interface illustrates routes, delivery statuses, and possible reassignment. It does not provide operational GPS tracking or demonstrate improvements for a real fleet.

## From demonstration to project

A real application requires agreement on scope, data, permissions, integrations, tests, and operating conditions. Technology and timelines are defined around those needs.
