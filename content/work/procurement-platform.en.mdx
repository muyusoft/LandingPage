## The problem

Purchase requests moved through spreadsheets and email chains across three manufacturing sites. Approvals routinely took four to six business days, and nobody had a reliable view of what was pending, approved, or stuck.

## What we built

A web platform where requests, approvals, and vendor records live in one place. Role-based approval chains route each request automatically, and every site manager gets a live dashboard instead of a weekly export. We integrated with the client's existing ERP so purchase orders sync without manual re-entry.

## The result

Average approval time dropped from four days to under six hours. The finance team now closes the month without reconciling three separate spreadsheets, and the client's ops lead uses the audit trail directly during supplier negotiations.
