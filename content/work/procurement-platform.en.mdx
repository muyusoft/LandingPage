## The scenario

This example explores how to organize requests, suppliers, and approvals in a shared interface.

The interface illustrates request statuses, ownership, and a possible ERP connection. It is not an operational integration or a client delivery.

## From demonstration to project

A real application requires agreement on scope, data, permissions, integrations, tests, and operating conditions. Technology and timelines are defined around those needs.
